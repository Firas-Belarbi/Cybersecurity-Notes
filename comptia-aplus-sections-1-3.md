## 2. Section Two — Safety and Professionalism

This section covered essential guidelines for working as an IT technician:

### Tools of the Trade
Common tools introduced:
- Multimeter  
- ESD strap  
- Cable tester  
- Screwdrivers and standard hardware tools  

### Troubleshooting Theory
The CompTIA troubleshooting model:
1. Identify the problem  
2. Establish a theory of probable cause  
3. Test the theory  
4. Establish a plan of action  
5. Implement the solution  
6. Verify full system functionality  
7. Document findings
